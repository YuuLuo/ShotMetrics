Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

ShotMetrics ("we", "our", or "the app") is a precision shooting group analysis tool for Android developed by YuuLuo. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Do Not Collect

ShotMetrics is designed with privacy in mind. The app:

  • Does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services
  • Does not require an account or registration
  • Does not connect to the internet for any purpose
  • Does not access your location automatically

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data created within ShotMetrics is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it unless you explicitly choose to share or export. This includes:

  • Shooting sessions — impact point coordinates, caliber, distance, and calculated ballistic statistics are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Target images — photos taken with the camera or imported from your gallery are copied to the app's local storage for annotation purposes.
  • User preferences — settings such as default units, caliber, and display options are stored locally using Android DataStore.

You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Camera Permission

ShotMetrics requests access to your device's camera solely to allow you to take photos of shooting targets for analysis. Camera images are processed and stored locally on your device. No images are uploaded to any server.

Exported Files

When you choose to export data (annotated images or CSV files), the files are saved to your device's local storage or shared through Android's built-in sharing mechanism. The app does not transmit exported files to any external server.

Third-Party Services

ShotMetrics does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data. The app operates entirely offline.

Children's Privacy

ShotMetrics is not directed at children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
